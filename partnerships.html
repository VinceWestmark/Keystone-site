Why “Keystone”

Our name comes from our Colorado home and from the role of a keystone — the part every other part depends on for support.

Teamwork and collaboration are core values for Keystone Mission Solutions. We recognize the fastest way to deliver mission capability is to work across industry, bringing together existing products and expertise. Keystone bridges the gap between mission needs and industry capability to accelerate the development and delivery of new systems to US Government customers.

We also provide business consulting to strategic partners, with a goal of driving shared growth and success. These services complement our Engineering Services to help accelerate the growth of our partners.
